## What's new

- **November firmware support across S26 Ultra variants.** Matching regional and carrier builds use the existing automatic kernel-family selection. Includes checks for the Korean BZK1 identity and fresh profile selection after a firmware update.
- **Refreshed root backends.** Updated KernelSU and KernelSU-Next as matched driver/daemon pairs. BakaSU and KowSU were checked and remain current at their recorded revisions.
- **Repaired backend builds.** Fixed the Rust errors that blocked refreshes while preserving upstream profile-name validation.

The tested SusFS module and Open Redirect fix from 1.3.8 are retained. Mountify remains unsupported.

November compatibility is supported by the earlier Korean boot-image inspection and the maintainer's reports of successful DFRpot tests. This RMGU build and every regional/backend combination have not had a new phone test.

## Updating

1. Install **RootMyGalaxyUltra-1.4.0.apk** over the existing app.
2. Perform a **full reboot**.
3. Activate root again and enable SusFS.
