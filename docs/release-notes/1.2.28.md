### Samsung SusFS dispatcher adaptation

- Prefer the syscall-table route for SusFS commands and retain the Samsung kprobe fallback when its entry checks pass.
- Share command admission and deferred-work ownership between both routes; preserve initialization and unload safeguards.
- Count the complete table wrapper, including normal reboot forwarding and sleeping original calls.
- Refuse fallback on an explicit BTI entry or when the entry cannot be inspected; report the selected route through a root-readable parameter.
- Include the rebuilt Android16/6.12 module, complete source, focused patch and validation records.

Host regression tests, real-kernel-header syscall CFI checks and the ARM64 build passed. The maintainer reports that the signed dispatcher test works on their phone. **Fully reboot after installing this release before loading the bundled drivers and SusFS module.**

[Implementation and validation details](https://github.com/xorpix/Root-My-Galaxy-Ultra/blob/main/docs/susfs-dispatcher-20261029.md).
